The Android™ NDK and Arm® GNU Toolchain are composite distributions. Their archives include
component-specific license and notice files that define the applicable license terms.

This inventory is not a substitute for any license or notice files required by the components
included in a distribution.

The prebuilt dependency source commits and archive checksums are pinned in
[`cmake/deps/dependencies.json`](cmake/deps/dependencies.json). They correspond to the upstream
revisions listed above. The prebuilt archives include upstream license texts. MNN, ONNX Runtime,
Protocol Buffers, and SentencePiece notices were added from those pinned revisions under
`share/licenses/<library>/`; existing ExecuTorch and LiteRT notices are retained. Complete the
redistribution review of all bundled components before publishing these archives publicly.
